## Design Context

### Users
Price-conscious travelers and travel planners in evaluation mode — comparing tradeoffs across multiple itineraries (price vs. duration vs. stops) before booking. Often under mild time pressure, on desktop or mobile, sometimes multitasking. Goal: answer "what is the best-value trip?" quickly and confidently.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: precise, practical, composed.
Tone: sharp comparison tool, not a glossy booking funnel. Feels like a financial/productivity tool applied to travel — information hierarchy drives everything.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark mode only (dark blue-grey base, warm amber/orange accent)
- Dense but readable — utility-led, not decorative
- Strong numeric hierarchy — prices, durations, times are the heroes
- Calm, controlled motion — state changes only, no decoration
- Anti-references: consumer travel affiliates, bright booking funnels, playful lifestyle brands, dashboard overloaded with cards and badges

### Design Principles
1. **Comparison over decoration** — every element must help users evaluate tradeoffs.
2. **Numeric hierarchy** — prices, durations, stop counts drive visual weight.
3. **Legibility at a glance** — current best action and active constraints always visible.
4. **Motion reinforces state** — transitions communicate change, never distract.
5. **Mobile preserves decision quality** — don't stack desktop sections; adapt for evaluation on the go.
